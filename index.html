About Me

I'm a first-year Computer Science student at the University of the Cordilleras. I use Python, Java, and analytics to find patterns in data and turn them into tools that are simple and genuinely useful to the people who use them.

Alongside my studies, I've spent three years as a training coordinator at a private school and run my own tutoring initiatives, turning topics from coding logic to math and science into clear, bite-sized lessons. That experience taught me to explain complex ideas simply, a skill I'm excited to bring to reporting and presenting data.

Outside class, I explore linguistics and build digital study spaces. I learn fast, stay curious, and I'm ready to grow into an analyst who makes insights easy for anyone to understand.
