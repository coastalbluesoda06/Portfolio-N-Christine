I am a 20-year-old Computer Science student at the University of the Cordilleras who loves finding patterns in data. I use Python, Java, and analytics to build tools that are not only powerful under the hood, but also simple and genuinely helpful for people to use every day.

Beyond my studies, I bring three years of experience as a training coordinator at a private school, alongside running my own educational tutoring initiatives. This background helps me break down tricky concepts—from coding logic to math and science—into bite-sized, engaging lessons that make learning feel effortless for others.

Whether I am writing code, exploring linguistics, or crafting digital study spaces, I love blending technical thinking with a clean, creative touch. I am still growing every single day, and I am excited to build a career in data analytics that inspires others and keeps curiosity at the heart of it all.
